STORYCRAFT DOSSIER — static copy of the site

1. Unzip the archive into any folder.
2. Open index.html with a double click. It opens in the browser. No server and no PHP.
3. Edit copy, name, links, and media paths in index.html (Sublime, VS Code, any editor).
4. Colors, type, and motion live in css/styles.css.
5. The portfolio player lives in js/player.js.
6. Put your own photos and videos in the media/ folder. In index.html, search for:
   INSERT YOUR VIDEO URL HERE
   INSERT YOUR IMAGE URL HERE
   INSERT YOUR BACKGROUND VIDEO URL HERE
   INSERT YOUR SOCIAL URLS HERE

Syne / Outfit / IBM Plex Mono load from Google Fonts when you are online.
Offline, the page still opens on system fonts.
